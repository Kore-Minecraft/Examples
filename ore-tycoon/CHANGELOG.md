## 1.0.0

First release of **Ore Tycoon**, for Minecraft Java 26.2.

- A floating island factory built above `x=0 z=0`, reached from the chat or the pause screen menu.
- A giant core to mine by hitting or right-clicking it, evolving through 8 levels up to a beacon.
- 7 drill stations mining coins every second, 25 copies each, 15% more expensive every copy.
- 5 pickaxes at the forge, the best ones adding a share of the production to each click.
- Golden nuggets every minute or so: 30 seconds of production or a ×3 frenzy.
- Prestige at the altar for a permanent +25% bonus per star, up to 20 stars.
- Holograms, sidebar and boss bar with shortened numbers such as `12.3K`.
- Play and Leave bring players back where they came from.
- Operator commands to give coins, spawn a nugget, rebuild, reset and uninstall.
